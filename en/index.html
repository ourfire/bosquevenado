We believe the real resilience of a community, and better yet its antifragility, lies in the ties among its members and between the community and its local context: Acanceh and its people. Those ties make us stronger than any wall, fence or weapon in the kind of collapse many people predict today.

Your stake

What your stake gives you, and what it doesn't.

Your stake is a set of shares in a series of La Tokenizadora's SAPI de C.V., represented by a token. It gives you lifetime use of one or more 200 m² lots and a say in how the land is cared for.

Included

  • Lifetime use of your lotsFrom a single 200 m² lot, or a cluster of 2, 4 or 8, in phase 3
  • Shared water, power and foodWell, solar hub, green lanes and food forest, with a maintenance fee
  • Building on your lotsType, materials and size set by the rules: to be defined
  • A vote on decisions about the landRules, improvements and admission of new members
  • Inheritance and resaleYour right passes to your heirs or to a buyer, with admission by the community

Not included

  • An individual deedThe land belongs to the company; your right is recorded in the share register
  • Clearing land on your ownAny clearing is decided together and with permits
  • Renting or commercial useRule: to be defined

Structure

The land, the company and the token.

We would rather put money into water, power and food than into paperwork, without giving up rigor: every stake is backed by shares.

The land

The property belongs to a share series of La Tokenizadora's SAPI de C.V., created for Bosque Venado. No one can split it up or sell it in pieces.

Your stake

It is a set of shares in that series, entered in the share register as soon as you sign. That gives you legal certainty right away, without a deed for each lot or notary fees on every transaction.

The token

It represents your shares and makes them easy to move: you can pass them on, pledge them as collateral or transfer them. It is not anonymous: the community approves every change of holder.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What does pre-sale mean?

That the shared infrastructure is being built with the first stakes. That is why the price is lower now and goes up 5% every time 4 lots are sold.

Why is there no deed for each lot?

Because the land belongs to the company and your right lives in your shares. That means no notary fees on each purchase, inheritance or transfer, and legal certainty from the moment you are entered in the share register.

Can I buy a single lot?

Yes, from one 200 m² lot. Clusters of 2, 4 or 8 are for people who want more space or to buy with friends or family; each person holds their own shares and the rules set use inside the cluster.

If I buy a back lot, how do I drive in?

Every back lot has its own access: the green lane next to its cluster, with two concrete wheel tracks for driving in at walking pace, the 3 m easement in clusters of 8, the south street or the power and water hub. On the plan, each lot shows its access.

Can I build?

Yes, under the building rules, which set type, materials and size: to be defined.

Can I sell, pass on or pledge my stake?

Yes. Your right of use transfers with your shares, on the terms set by the bylaws. The new holder goes through the same admission process you did.

Is there water and power?

There is a well with water at 27 meters. There is no grid connection: one of the lived-in houses runs on solar panels and batteries, and the power and water hub serves the shared areas.

How much is the maintenance fee?

MXN $50 a year per lot. It covers the street, water, shared power and forest care, and is adjusted to what the community needs.

What happens if I stop paying?

What happens to your payments and your stake is set in the contract: rule to be defined.
