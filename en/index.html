What your stake gives you, and what it doesn't.

Your stake is a set of shares in a series of La Tokenizadora's SAPI de C.V., represented by a token. It gives you lifetime use of a 200 m² lot and a say in how the land is cared for.

Included

  • Lifetime use of a 200 m² lotAssigned to your stake, in phase 3
  • Access to common areasRoads, water, power, garden and storage, with a maintenance fee
  • Building on your lotType, materials and size set by the rules: to be defined
  • A vote on decisions about the landRules, improvements and admission of new members
  • Inheritance and resaleYour right passes to your heirs or to a buyer, with admission by the community

Not included

  • An individual deed to the lotThe land belongs to the company; your right is recorded in the share register
  • Clearing land on your ownAny clearing is decided together and with permits
  • Renting or commercial use of the lotRule: to be defined
  • A promise of returns or appreciationThe value is in the use and the community

Your rights are set by the bylaws, the use rules and your contract. Final documents: to be defined.

How to join the community.

Before any payment, we get to know each other. A strong community starts with choosing well who joins.

  1. Visit the land

    Walk the forest, the buildings and the available lots.

  2. Talk with the community

    We discuss values, expectations and how you want to use the place.

  3. Reserve and review

    You receive the bylaws, rules and contract to review at your own pace.

  4. Sign and register

    You sign your contract and are entered in the share register.

  5. Receive your token

    La Tokenizadora issues your token and your lot is assigned.

The land, the company and the token.

Three pieces that back each other up, so your stake is clear today and stays clear if something fails.

The land

The property belongs to a share series of La Tokenizadora's SAPI de C.V., created for Bosque Venado. No one can split it up or sell it in pieces.

Your stake

It is a set of shares in that series. The share register is the controlling record, and your lot is tied to it.

The token

La Tokenizadora tokenizes each stake. Your token is your key, and if the network ever fails, the register and the rules also exist on paper.

Frequently asked questions.

Can I build on my lot?

Yes, under the building rules, which set type, materials and size: to be defined.

Do I get a deed to the lot?

No. The land belongs to the company. Your right of use is tied to your shares in the share register and to your contract.

Can I sell or pass on my stake?

Yes. The right of use transfers with your shares, by inheritance or sale. The new holder goes through the same admission process you did.

Is there water and power?

There is a well with water at 27 meters. There is no grid connection: one of the lived-in houses runs on solar panels and batteries, and the shared plan adds solar power for the community.

What about phases 1 and 2?

They are already sold. Phase 3 is the only one for sale.

How much is the maintenance fee?

It covers roads, water, power and forest care. Amount: to be defined.

What happens if I stop paying?

What happens to your payments and your stake is set in the contract: rule to be defined.
