The community

An intentional community, not a subdivision.

Bosque Venado exists to build community around a solidarity economy and resilience in an uncertain future. We want water, power, food and real ties between neighbors. We are in pre-sale: every stake funds what we share.

Water, power and food

The basics, solved together: well, sun and planting. It is what keeps us going if things outside fail.

Solidarity economy

We share tools, work and harvests. We would rather put money into the land and the community than into paperwork.

Trust among peers

We all know each other. The community approves who joins and who leaves; no one takes part anonymously.

Legal rigor

Trust does not mean improvising. Everything is tokenized and backed by shares in a series of a SAPI de C.V., with bylaws and rules.

Your stake

What your stake gives you, and what it doesn't.

Your stake is a set of shares in a series of La Tokenizadora's SAPI de C.V., represented by a token. It gives you lifetime use of a cluster of 2, 4 or 8 lots and a say in how the land is cared for.

Included

  • Lifetime use of your clusterOf 2, 4 or 8 lots of 200 m², in phase 3

Structure

The land, the company and the token.

We would rather put money into water, power and food than into paperwork. But trust among peers does not mean giving up rigor: every stake is backed by shares.

The land

The property belongs to a share series of La Tokenizadora's SAPI de C.V., created for Bosque Venado. No one can split it up or sell it in pieces.

Your stake

It is a set of shares in that series, entered in the share register as soon as you sign. That gives you legal certainty right away, without a deed for each lot or notary fees on every transaction.

The token

It represents your shares and makes them easy to move: you can pass them on, pledge them as collateral or transfer them. It is not anonymous: the community approves every change of holder.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What does pre-sale mean?

That the shared infrastructure is being built with the first stakes. That is why the price is lower now and goes up 5% every time 4 lots are sold.

Why is there no deed for each lot?

Because the land belongs to the company and your right lives in your shares. That means no notary fees on each purchase, inheritance or transfer, and legal certainty from the moment you are entered in the share register.

Can I pledge my stake as collateral?

Yes, your shares can be pledged on the terms set by the bylaws. The token makes it easier, but any change of holder goes through the community.

Why clusters and not single lots?

Each cluster has a row of lots at the front, on the street, and another at the back, next to the green lane. Bought together, no lot is left without access, and there is room for fruit trees between clusters.

How do I drive to the back lots?

Along the green lane next to each cluster: it has two concrete wheel tracks for driving in at walking pace. Clusters of 8 also have a 3 m easement through the middle.

Can I buy a cluster with friends or family?

Yes. A cluster of 4 or 8 can be shared; each person holds their own shares and the rules set access and use inside the cluster.

Can I build?

Yes, under the building rules, which set type, materials and size: to be defined.

Can I sell or pass on my stake?

Yes. The right of use transfers with your shares, by inheritance or sale. The new holder goes through the same admission process you did.

Is there water and power?

There is a well with water at 27 meters. There is no grid connection: one of the lived-in houses runs on solar panels and batteries, and the power and water hub serves the shared areas.

What about phases 1 and 2?

They are already sold. Phase 3 is the only one for sale.

How much is the maintenance fee?

It covers the street, water, shared power and forest care. Amount: to be defined.

What happens if I stop paying?

What happens to your payments and your stake is set in the contract: rule to be defined.
